Sports & Athletics Facilities

Chaitanya Science and Arts College believes in the all-round development of its students. Physical education and sports form an integral part of our curriculum. The college boasts excellent sports infrastructure, state-of-the-art facilities, and training programs that encourage students to participate in indoor and outdoor sports at regional, state, and national levels.

Outdoor Sports Ground

A multi-purpose sports arena for Cricket, Football, and Athletics with a standard running track.

Indoor Games Arena

Dedicated indoor facilities for Table Tennis, Chess, Carrom, and Badminton.

Fitness Center & Gym

Well-equipped gymnasium with modern strength training and cardio machines for students and staff.

Annual Sports Events
  • Annual Athletic Meet: Inter-departmental athletics challenges.
  • Chaitanya Trophy: Inter-collegiate Cricket and Volleyball tournament.
  • Yoga Day Celebrations: Campus-wide training session on International Yoga Day.
Sports Policies & Benefits
  • Sports quota admissions for outstanding regional/national players.
  • Cash incentives and fee concessions for tournament winners.
  • Special academic support and attendance relief during tournaments.
